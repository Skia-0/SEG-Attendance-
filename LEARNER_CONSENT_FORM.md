### Consent Declaration

I, the undersigned learner, acknowledge and agree to the following:

1. **Attendance Tracking:** My attendance at SEG hub sessions will be 
   recorded digitally using the SEG Attendance System.

2. **Verification Methods:** I understand that my attendance may be 
   verified using one or more of the following methods:
   - NFC card tap (a card assigned to me during registration)
   - Fingerprint scan (using the coordinator's device sensor)
   - Manual verification by the coordinator (with documented reason)

3. **Data Collected:** The system will record:
   - My name and SEG ID
   - Check-in and check-out times for each session
   - The verification method used
   - My NFC card identifier (if applicable)

4. **Fingerprint Privacy:** I understand that my fingerprint data is 
   processed ONLY on the coordinator's phone hardware and is NEVER 
   stored on any server or database. Only a success/failure result 
   is recorded.

5. **NFC Card:** If issued an NFC card:
   - The card contains only a hardware serial number
   - No personal data is stored on the card itself
   - I will return the card at the end of the cohort
   - If lost, I must report it to my coordinator immediately

6. **Certification:** My attendance record will be used to determine 
   whether I meet the minimum attendance threshold required for 
   certification at the end of the cohort.

7. **Data Access:** My attendance data may be viewed by:
   - My hub coordinator
   - SEG central office administrators
   - No external third parties

8. **My Rights:** Under the Ghana Data Protection Act 2012, I have 
   the right to access, correct, or request deletion of my personal 
   data by contacting my hub coordinator or SEG central office.
